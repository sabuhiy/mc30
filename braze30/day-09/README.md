# Day 9 - Grow Fenly's List with a Landing Page

## English

Build Fenly's market sign-up page and its confirmation page, then bring in the first two weeks of sign-ups and watch the double opt-in campaign fire.

**Files:**

- `fenly-market-signups-week1.csv`: 5 market sign-ups (FNL-100201 to FNL-100205), 10 columns: external_id, first_name, last_name, email, email_subscribe, home_city, time_zone, diet_preference, signup_source, signup_date
- `fenly-market-signups-week2.csv`: 5 more market sign-ups (FNL-100206 to FNL-100210), same columns

Import them with Audience > Import Users > Import users > Attributes, after you launch the confirmation campaign from [Day 8](../day-08/). The page copy and the diet choices are on the challenge page. The logo is `fenly-logo.png` from [Day 3](../day-03/).
